### Calculus:

#### Books:

* [Differential and Integral Calculus Volume 1 - N. Piskunov](https://archive.org/details/piskunov-differential-and-integral-calculus-volume-1-mir) [Book/PDF]
* [Differential and Integral Calculus Volume 2 - N. Piskunov](https://archive.org/details/piskunov-differential-and-integral-calculus-volume-2-mir) [Book/PDF]

#### Courses/Videos:

* [MIT Single Variable Calculus (18.01) - MIT OCW](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-01-single-variable-calculus-fall-2006/download/) [Course + Notes]
* [Differential Equations - 3Blue1Brown](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDNPOjrT6KVlfJuKtYTftqH6) [YT Playlist]

The Piskunov books are classic rigorous calculus textbooks with lots of worked examples and exercises. MIT OCW provides structured lecture notes, assignments, and exams, while 3Blue1Brown’s differential equations playlist is excellent for intuition and visualization.

### Linear Algebra:

#### Courses/Videos:

* [MIT 18.06 Linear Algebra (Spring 2005) - Gilbert Strang](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLE7DDD91010BC51F8) [YT Playlist]

Gilbert Strang’s MIT 18.06 course is one of the most widely recommended introductions to linear algebra, especially for understanding concepts geometrically and computationally.

### Statistics & Probability:

#### Interactive Resources:

* [Seeing Theory - Brown University](https://seeing-theory.brown.edu/) [Interactive Website]

Seeing Theory is a visual and interactive introduction to probability and statistics, particularly useful for building intuition behind statistical concepts.
